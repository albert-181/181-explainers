Amstel 1 beat sequencer

Proof of concept. Each drum hit becomes light on the white roof beams. The kick sends a red pulse down the street, frame by frame.

Output map: 26 lit frames × 2 beams, the pattern that would go out as Art-Net

Pattern memories

Click an empty slot to store the current pattern, click a stored slot to recall it, X to clear it.

Set a track to fire every 2, 4 or 8 bars to place a kick only on that bar. Sound is synthesised in the browser, it is not a sample of any real machine. Schematic only, not to scale for photometry.
